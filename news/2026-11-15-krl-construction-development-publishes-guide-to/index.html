The guide is for people beginning to plan a custom home and weighing what they want from a project. It covers practical considerations such as the property, the homeowner’s goals, materials and budget. Thinking through those points can help someone prepare for a first conversation with a builder and describe the work they have in mind.

Those details also matter when setting a project scope and estimate. KRL says it discusses a client’s property, goals, materials and budget to develop a clear scope and estimate. Readers can use the guide as a starting point for organizing those questions before contacting a contractor. It may be useful now for people comparing options or getting ready to discuss a project, though the guide does not replace a project-specific review or estimate.
